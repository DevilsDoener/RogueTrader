The introductory rules on mutations ("Mutations," "Gaining Mutations,"
"Mutation") physically remain on PDF page 372 per the binding page split and
are therefore captured at the end of `14-Traits.md`. This file contains the
full random table **Table 14-3: Mutations**, which is referenced there
(1d100, duplicate results are always re-rolled).

## Table 14-3: Mutations

| Roll (1d100) | Mutation |
|---|---|
| 01–05 | **Grotesque:** The mutant is either horribly deformed, scarred, or bestial, marking him as cursed and unclean. He suffers a −20 penalty on Fellowship Tests when dealing with "normals," but gains a +10 bonus on Intimidate Tests. |
| 06–10 | **Tough Hide:** Thanks to dense skin and thick scar tissue, the mutant gains the Natural Armour (2) Trait. |
| 11–15 | **Misshapen:** The mutant's spine and limbs are grotesquely twisted. He can no longer Run and reduces his Agility by 2d10. |
| 16–20 | **Feels No Pain:** The mutant scarcely notices injury or pain. He gains +5 Wounds and the Iron Jaw Talent. |
| 21–25 | **Brute:** The mutant is physically powerful, with deformed, slab-like muscle mass. Increase Strength and Toughness by 10 each. |
| 26–30 | **Nightsider:** The mutant gains keen eyesight and can see clearly in areas of poor illumination. He gains the Dark-Sight Trait, but suffers a −10 penalty on all Tests in areas of bright light unless his eyes and skin are shielded or covered. |
| 31–35 | **Mental Regressive:** The mutant is mentally stunted. Roll 1d10 separately for Intelligence, Perception, Willpower, and Fellowship: on 1–5 the characteristic is reduced by 1d10; on 6–7 the characteristic is halved; on 8–9 no change; on 10 the characteristic is reduced to 5. The mutant also gains 1d10 Insanity Points. |
| 36–40 | **Malformed Hands:** The mutant's hands fuse into slab-like appendages. Weapon Skill and Ballistic Skill are reduced by 10. He also suffers a −20 penalty on any Test requiring fine motor skills. |
| 41–45 | **Tox Blood:** The mutant's system is saturated with toxic pollutants and poisonous chemicals. He gains a +20 bonus on Toughness Tests against poison, but reduces Toughness and Intelligence by 1d10 each. If the mutant's blood comes into contact with a living creature, that creature must pass a Difficult (−10) Toughness Test or suffer 1d10 points of damage, ignoring Armour and Toughness. |
| 46–50 | **Hulking:** The mutant grows in stature and body mass. He gains the Hulking Size Trait, increases his Strength characteristic by 10, and gains +5 Wounds. |
| 51–55 | **Wyrdling:** The mutant possesses minor psychic powers he has managed to conceal until now. He gains the Psy Rating 2 Talent. If he already has this Talent, he gains the next higher Psy Rating Talent he does not yet possess. The mutant also gains 2 Psychic Techniques of his choice from the Discipline of his choice. |
| 56–59 | **Vile Deformity:** The mutant bears a terrible deformity showing the touch of the Warp and which should not, by rights, exist in a rational universe — from writhing tentacles in place of arms, to skinless glistening flesh, to rearranged facial features, to thousands of restless eyes across the body, to name but a few. The mutant gains the Fear (1) Trait. |
| 60–63 | **Aberration:** The mutant becomes a bizarre hybrid of human and animal (reptile, insect, or other beast). He increases Strength and Agility by 10 each, reduces Intelligence by −1d10, and Fellowship by −10. |
| 64–67 | **Degenerate Mind:** The mutant's mind is twisted and inhuman. Intelligence and Fellowship are each reduced by −1d10. Additionally, roll 1d10: on 1–3 he gains the Frenzy Talent; on 4–7 the Fearless Talent; on 8–0 the From Beyond Trait. |
| 68–71 | **Ravaged Body:** The mutant's body has been completely reshaped by the Warp. Roll 1d5 times on this table. Such mutations nonetheless show, regardless of their nature, the unmistakable touch of Chaos. |
| 71–74 | **Clawed/Fanged:** The mutant grows razor claws, a fanged maw, barbed flesh, or some other form of natural weapon. He gains the Natural Weapon Trait (either I or R damage). |
| 75–78 | **Necrophage:** The mutant gains +10 to Toughness and the Regeneration Trait, but must feed on copious amounts of raw flesh or starve. |
| 79–81 | **Corrupted Flesh:** Beneath the mutant's skin a blasphemous transformation has taken place, in which living organs have been replaced with writhing creatures and blood with rank, maggot-infested filth. If the mutant suffers Critical Damage, he gains Fear (2) for that round. |
| 82–85 | **Venomous:** The mutant's natural attacks are poisonous. He gains the Toxic Trait. |
| 86–89 | **Hideous Strength:** The mutant gains the Unnatural Strength Trait. |
| 90–91 | **Multiple Appendages:** The mutant has developed additional functional limbs in the form of arms, tentacles, or a prehensile tail (or several tails). He gains the Ambidextrous and Two Weapon Wielder Talents, as well as a +10 bonus on Climb Tests and Grapple attacks. |
| 92–93 | **Worm:** The mutant's lower limbs have fused into a worm- or snake-like tail. He gains 5 additional Wounds and the Crawler Trait. |
| 94 | **Nightmarish:** So twisted and terrible is the mutant's appearance that it sends enemies fleeing in terror. He gains the Fear (3) Trait. |
| 95 | **Malleable:** The mutant possesses a repulsively fluid flexibility and can stretch and flatten his body. Increase Agility by 10. He gains a +20 bonus on Climb Tests and Grapple attacks. He can also squeeze through openings only a quarter of his usual body size. |
| 96 | **Winged:** The mutant's body has warped to grow a pair of leathery wings or similar. He gains the Flyer Trait with a value equal to AB×2. |
| 97 | **Corpulent:** The mutant's massive, bloated frame gives him +5 Wounds and the Unnatural Toughness Trait. He can no longer take the Run action. |
| 98 | **Shadow Kin:** The mutant now has only a tenuous hold on our reality and, though gaunt and haggard, can partially shift into the Warp at will. He gains the Phase Trait and reduces Strength and Toughness by 10 each. |
| 99 | **Corrosive Bile:** The mutant can vomit forth burning bile, flesh-eating maggots, or some other horrific substance instead of attacking normally in melee. The mutant must test Ballistic Skill to use this mutation. Using it is a full action. The attack can be dodged but not parried. On a successful Test, the attack deals 1d10+2 R (or E) Tearing damage. |
| 00 | **Hellspawn:** Suffused with the energies of the Warp, the mutant is more daemon than man. He gains the Daemonic, Fear (2), and From Beyond Traits. |
