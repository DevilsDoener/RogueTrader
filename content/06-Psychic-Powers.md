# Chapter VI: Psychic Powers

> Source: *Rogue Trader Core Rulebook*, book pages 153–172 / PDF pages 157–176.
> Flavour text is condensed; rules values, technique prerequisites and random tables are preserved as a full reference.

## Psykers and Psychic Ability

Imperial society distinguishes, among others, Astropaths, Sanctioned Psykers, Navigators, Latent and Renegade Psykers, Sorcerers, Xenos Psykers, and Untouchables. For player characters in this rulebook, the soul-bound **Astropath Transcendent** is the most relevant. He begins with **Psy Rating 2** and can increase it through Advances.

- An Astropath Transcendent gains +20 Willpower against Possession, in Opposed Willpower Tests against Daemons, and against Talents, Psychic Powers, or other effects of daemonic origin.
- When rolling on Perils of the Warp, he rolls an additional `d10` and may discard one of the dice.
- Untouchables have no warp signature and dampen Psychic Activity.

Psychic Techniques belong to Disciplines. Any psyker with Psy Rating 1+ may access one Discipline; a psyker can master a maximum of **three** Disciplines at once (more only under special circumstances or at the GM's discretion). An Astropath begins with Telepathy and **Astral Telepathy**, chooses two further Techniques at character creation, and acquires more via the Psychic Technique Talent. A psyker who possesses eight Techniques of one Discipline achieves **Discipline Mastery**: Fettered Psy Rating for that Discipline +1; Unfettered and Push remain unchanged.

## Psychic Strength and Focus Power

Every Technique begins with the stated **Focus Power Action**. The Focus Power Score gains **+5 per Psy Rating spent**. A result of 100 is always a failure.

### Table 6-1: Psychic Strength

| Strength | Effective Psy Rating | Astropath/Sanctioned Psyker | Renegade Psyker/Sorcerer |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Fettered | half Psy Rating, rounded up | no Phenomena check | roll on Psychic Phenomena on Doubles in the Focus Power Test |
| Unfettered | full Psy Rating | roll on Psychic Phenomena on Doubles in the Focus Power Test | roll on Doubles and +10 on Psychic Phenomena |
| Push | Psy Rating +1 to +3/+4 | always Psychic Phenomena; +5 per additional Rating, max. +15 | always Psychic Phenomena; +10 per additional Rating, max. +40 |

Sanctioned Psykers may add at most +3 when pushing, Renegades/Sorcerers at most +4. Multiple sources that raise Effective Psy Rating do not stack; only the highest applies. Values above 10 are possible.

### General Rules

- **No Test:** An Unfettered Technique used without a test does not trigger Phenomena; Push always triggers it.
- **Multiple Powers:** Two Powers sustained simultaneously each lower Effective Psy Rating by 2, three by 3, and so on. If one drops to 0, it ends. On Phenomena, +10 to the roll per additional sustained Power.
- **Cumulative Effects:** Modifiers, Characteristic Bonuses, and other benefits from Psychic Techniques do not stack; only the highest applies.
- **Range/Line of Sight:** Unless stated otherwise, the psyker must see or otherwise perceive the target, and it must be in range.
- **Multiple Targets:** The psyker rolls once; each target makes its Opposed Test against that result.
- **Detection:** Psykers may use Psyniscience to perceive active Techniques and their source.
- **Unnatural Willpower:** The multiplier is added to Psy Rating and to successes in Opposed Tests involving Psychic Powers.
- **Power Scale:** All effect descriptions of the Techniques assume **Unfettered Strength**, regardless of which Psychic Strength (Fettered/Unfettered/Push) the Technique is actually being used at.

## Telepathy Discipline

**Activation:** Free Action · **Maintainable:** Yes · **Range:** 1 km × Psy Rating · **Focus:** none. At PR 1–2 only words, at PR 3–4 also dream-like black-and-white images, at PR 5–6 clear coloured multisensory images, at PR 7+ using it above Fettered without careful modulation feels like screaming.

**Basic Technique – Thought Sending:** to selected visible/known minds or as a broadcast; without a shared language, -20. Unwilling targets resist with Opposed Willpower.

### Mind Probe (Table 6-6)

| Round | Information |
|---:|---|
| 1 – Contact | name, mood, Insanity, physical condition |

### Table 6-7: Telepathic Domination Techniques

| Technique | Focus | Sustain | XP | Test | Effect |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Inspire | Half Action | Yes | 100 | Willpower | up to PR targets within 2 m × PR overcome Pinning and gain +10 against Fear |
| Delude | Half Action | Yes | 100 | Opposed WP | +30 Interaction against a target within 1 m × PR; no self-harm or true mind control |

Prerequisites: Compel ← Delude; Sensory Deprivation ← Compel; Dominate ← Sensory Deprivation; Puppet Master ← Dominate; Reprogram ← Dominate + Mind Probe.

**Puppet Master (Detail):** On success, the psyker's mind leaves his own body (which remains helpless, as if in deep sleep) and completely takes over the target; the target's consciousness is pushed into a nightmarish dream state. The possessed target retains its own **physical** Characteristics (WS, BS, Strength, Toughness, Agility) but takes on the psyker's **mental** Characteristics (Intelligence, Willpower, Fellowship). Within the borrowed body, the psyker may use either the target's own Skills or his own, each at **-10**. Suicidal actions force a new Opposed Willpower Test (this time the **target** bears the -10, not the psyker). Voluntarily ending the Power costs `1d5` Fatigue Levels. If the possessed body is killed, the psyker is violently thrown back: `1d5` Wounds (ignoring any defence/armour) plus `1d10` Insanity Points. If the distance between psyker and target exceeds the Technique's range, the connection breaks and the psyker returns to his own body, also suffering `1d5` Fatigue Levels.

**Reprogram (Detail):** If the target wins the Opposed Willpower Test, it fends off the reprogramming and throws the psyker out; the procedure ends, the psyker gains one Fatigue level, and may not attempt the Technique again for at least `1d5` hours.

### Reprogram (Table 6-8)

| Degrees of Success | Effect |
|---:|---|
| 1 – Sow Confusion | obscure one memory/event; -20 to recall it |
| 2 – Implant Falsehood | implant a simple false piece of information up to one sentence long |
| 3 – Rewritten History | rewrite one recent event or sequence of events |
| 4 – Sculpt Synapses | replace/erase one major long-term memory; target gains `1d5` Insanity |

## Telekinesis Discipline

**Activation:** Half Action · **Maintainable:** Yes · **Range:** up to 5 m × PR · **Focus:** Willpower/Opposed Willpower.

**Basic Technique – Mind over Matter:**

- lifts/moves inanimate objects up to 10 kg × PR slowly within range;
- hurls objects up to 5 kg × PR after a Ballistic Skill Test: `1d10 I` +1 Damage per 5 kg;
- knocks a target Prone and pushes it back PR metres after Opposed Willpower vs. Strength.

## Compatibility with Dark Heresy

- Psychic Techniques and Psychic Powers are treated as rules-identical; Resistance works both ways.
- Psy Rating is directly compatible.
- Every +4 Power Threshold in *Dark Heresy* corresponds to roughly -1 Psy Rating here.
- Every +1 on Power Rolls corresponds to +5 on Focus Power Tests.
- Untouchable lowers the Psy Rating of every psyker within 5 m by 5.
- Sorcery remains unchanged, but uses the Sorcerer row of the Psychic Strength table.
- Daemons and Warp Entities with Daemonic/Stuff of Nightmares use only Unfettered; Phenomena occur at GM's discretion, but they themselves suffer neither its drawbacks nor Perils.
