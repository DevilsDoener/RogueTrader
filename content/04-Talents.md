# Chapter IV: Talents

> Source: Rogue Trader Core Rulebook, Chapter IV, book pages 89–108 (PDF pages 93–112).
> **Status: chapter fully captured.**

## Fundamentals

While **Skills** are acquired through learning and training, **Talents** are abilities born of innate traits and experience gathered over time.

Difference from Skills: a Talent requires **no Action and no Test**. Talents are **always active**; many grant bonuses to Tests or allow actions ordinary people cannot perform.

## Gaining Talents
Talents are gained at character creation (Chapter I) or chosen afterwards, mainly by spending Experience Points along the Career Path (Chapter II).

## Talent Groups
Some Talents represent a general ability applied to a specific category. When choosing such a Talent, you pick an entry from the group — e.g. **Peer (Astropaths)**. Each time it is bought again, a **different** specialisation may be chosen (e.g. Peer (Adeptus Mechanicus) next time).

The group lists are **representative, not exhaustive**: an unlisted entry (the book's example: "Yeshar Clan") may also be chosen with GM approval.

**Universal:** Some Weapon Training Talents have a group called "Universal". It covers several different weapon types, allowing the character to use many weapon variants without penalty. It represents the broad, unusual background of most members of a Rogue Trader dynasty.

## Talent Prerequisites
Many Talents require something as a prerequisite — Characteristic values, Skills, other Talents, or even special abilities.

## Detailed Talent Descriptions

### Air of Authority
**Prerequisite:** Fellowship 30

A successful **Command Test** can affect `1d10 + Fellowship Bonus` targets. The Talent does not work on hostile targets and only affects NPCs.

### Ambidextrous
**Prerequisite:** Agility 30

The Explorer can use both hands equally well and does not suffer the usual −20 penalty for actions with his off hand. Together with a matching **Two-Weapon Wielder** Talent, the penalty for attacking with both weapons in the same turn drops to −10.

### Armour of Contempt
**Prerequisite:** Willpower 40

When the Explorer gains Corruption Points, he reduces the number gained by 1 (minimum 0). With a successful **Willpower Test** as a Free Action, he can ignore the effects of his accumulated Corruption Points for 1 Round.

### Assassin Strike
**Prerequisites:** Agility 40, Acrobatics

After a Melee Attack, the Explorer may use a successful **Acrobatics Test** as a Free Action to move his half movement rate. The opponent gets no Free Attack from this movement. Usable only once per Round.

### Autosanguine
The Explorer always counts as **Lightly Wounded** for medical treatment purposes and heals 2 Damage per day.

### Basic Weapon Training †
**Talent Groups:** Bolt, Las, Launcher, Primitive, SP, Universal

Weapons of the chosen group can be used without penalty. **Universal** covers Bolt, Las, Launcher, Melta, Plasma and SP. Without the matching Weapon Training, a −20 penalty applies to relevant Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill Tests.

### Bastion of Iron Will
**Prerequisites:** Psy Rating, Strong Minded, Willpower 40

Doubles the defensive **Psy Rating** on any Opposed Test involving Psyniscience or Psychic Techniques.

### Battle Rage
**Prerequisite:** Frenzy

The Explorer can Parry while **Frenzied**.

### Berserk Charge
On a **Charge Action**, the Explorer gains +20 to Weapon Skill instead of +10.

### Binary Chatter
The Explorer gains +10 on attempts to communicate with Servitors. Any ship he serves on gains +1 **Crew Morale** due to improved servitor efficiency.

### Blademaster
**Prerequisites:** Weapon Skill 30, Melee Weapon Training

When attacking with a bladed weapon — including chain weapons, power axes and power swords — the Explorer may reroll a missed attack once per Round.

### Blessed Radiance
**Prerequisites:** Purge the Unclean, Divine Ministration, The Emperor Protects **or** Wrath of the Righteous

- When the Explorer spends a Fate Point to activate **Pure Faith**, the immunity to Daemonic Presence extends to up to `2 × Willpower Bonus` targets.
- As a Free Action, he can lend a single Fate Point to an ally. If not spent, it returns at the end of the encounter.
- If he burns a Fate Point, he and up to `2 × Willpower Bonus` allies become immune to Daemonic Presence, Fear Tests and Corruption Points for the entire encounter. They also gain +10 on Tests against Psychic Attacks and other psychic manipulation, and take only half damage from psychic and Warp sources.

### Blind Fighting
**Prerequisite:** Perception 30

All penalties from restricted vision are halved, such as fighting in fog, smoke or darkness.

### Bloodtracker
If a wanted fugitive is turned in dead or alive for a bounty, the group gains +100 Objective Points for Military or Criminal Objectives.

### Bulging Biceps
**Prerequisite:** Strength 45

The Explorer can fire Heavy Weapons with Semi-Auto Burst or Full Auto Burst without bracing and does not suffer the usual −30 penalty.

### Catfall
**Prerequisite:** Agility 30

When falling, the Explorer may make an **Agility Test** as a Free Action. On success, each Degree of Success — including the first — reduces the distance used to determine falling damage by metres equal to his Agility Bonus.

### Chem Geld
Ordinary seduction attempts automatically fail. Charm Tests against the Explorer are one Difficulty level harder. Gaining this Talent causes 1 Insanity Point.

### Cleanse and Purify
**Prerequisite:** Flame Weapons Training (Universal)

Targets of the Explorer's flamer attacks suffer −20 on Agility Tests to avoid their effect.

### Combat Formation
**Prerequisite:** Intelligence 40

Before the Initiative roll, all other group members may use the Explorer's Intelligence Bonus instead of their own Agility Bonus for their Initiative.

### Combat Master
**Prerequisite:** Weapon Skill 30

Opponents gain no bonus in hand-to-hand combat for outnumbering the Explorer.

### Combat Sense
**Prerequisite:** Perception 40

The Explorer may use his Perception Bonus instead of his Agility Bonus for the Initiative roll.

### Concealed Cavity
A small compartment in the body or in augmetics can conceal an item up to about the size of a closed fist. It is found with a **Difficult (−10) Search Test**; using a Medicae scanner or Auspex, the Test is **Ordinary (+10)**.

### Counter Attack
**Prerequisite:** Weapon Skill 40

After a successful Parry, the Explorer may immediately attack with the parrying weapon as a Free Action; this attack suffers −20.

### Crack Shot
**Prerequisite:** Ballistic Skill 40

If a Ranged Attack causes Critical Damage, +2 Damage is added.

### Crippling Strike
**Prerequisite:** Weapon Skill 50

If a Melee Attack causes Critical Damage, +4 Damage is added.

### Crushing Blow
**Prerequisite:** Strength 40

Melee Attacks deal +2 Damage.

### Dark Soul
On **Malignancy Tests**, only half the normal penalty applies; details on Corruption are in Chapter X.

### Deadeye Shot
**Prerequisite:** Ballistic Skill 30

On a **Called Shot**, −10 applies instead of −20.

### Decadence
**Prerequisite:** Toughness 30

When drinking alcohol or similar beverages, the Explorer only becomes unconscious after failing Toughness Tests equal to twice his Toughness Bonus. He also gains +10 on Tests against Addiction.

### Die Hard
**Prerequisite:** Willpower 40

On Blood Loss, the Explorer may roll twice to avoid death.

### Disarm
**Prerequisite:** Agility 30

In Close Combat, the Explorer can perform an **Opposed Weapon Skill Test** as a Full Action. If he wins, the opponent's weapon falls at their feet. With at least 3 Degrees of Success, the Explorer takes the weapon for himself.

### Disturbing Voice
The Explorer gains +10 on Intimidate and Interrogation Tests, but suffers −10 on Fellowship Tests in non-threatening encounters, such as with animals, children, or easily frightened people.

### Divine Ministration
**Prerequisite:** Pure Faith

- On a Medicae Test, the Explorer may spend a Fate Point to restore Damage equal to his Willpower Bonus instead of the normal amount. This value is then normally added and multiplied depending on the treatment type (Medicae, p. 83). For First Aid on a Lightly Wounded character, `Willpower Bonus + Intelligence Bonus` Damage is healed.
- For a Fate Point, he removes all Fatigue from up to `2 × Willpower Bonus` people.
- He may burn a Fate Point so that someone who has just died is instead treated as Critically Wounded. Limits and eligibility are set by the GM; if the attempt fails, the Fate Point is not lost.

### Double Team
When attacking a target together, the Explorer gains an additional +10 on Weapon Skill Tests. If both attackers have the Talent, both gain a further +10, for a total of +20 in addition to the normal bonus for outnumbering.

### Dual Shot
**Prerequisites:** Agility 40, Two-Weapon Wielder (Ballistic)

With two pistols, the Explorer can fire both simultaneously as a Full Action. He makes a single Ballistic Skill Test; on success, both shots hit. Since both weapons count as one attack, an Aim Action for +10 or +20 may be taken beforehand, and a red-dot laser sight on one weapon gives +10. The usual −20 penalty for two weapons does not apply. Armour is applied to each hit separately, but Toughness only once against the combined Damage of both hits. A successful Dodge Test avoids both shots.

### Dual Strike
**Prerequisites:** Agility 40, Two-Weapon Wielder (Melee)

With two melee weapons, the Explorer can use both simultaneously as a Full Action. He makes a single Weapon Skill Test; on success, both weapons hit. Since both count as one attack, an Aim Action for +10 or +20 may be taken beforehand. The usual −20 penalty for two weapons does not apply. Armour is applied to each hit separately, but Toughness only once against the combined Damage. A successful Dodge or Parry Test avoids both hits.

### Duty Unto Death
**Prerequisite:** Willpower 45

During a fight, the Explorer can ignore the effects of Injury, Fatigue and Stunning. Damage is still inflicted, and death still functions normally.

### Electrical Succour
**Prerequisite:** Mechanicus Implants

In contact with a functioning, powered machine or a fully charged battery/power cell, the Explorer may make an **Ordinary (+10) Toughness Test**. Success removes 1 Fatigue plus 1 more per additional Degree of Success. Meditation and ritual invocation take 1 minute.

### Electro Graft Use
**Prerequisite:** Mechanicus Implants

While connected to a data port via his Electro Graft, the Explorer gains +10 on Common Lore, Inquiry and Tech-Use Tests.

### Enemy †
**Talent Groups:** Academics, Adepta Sororitas, Adeptus Arbites, Adeptus Mechanicus, Administratum, Astropaths, Ecclesiarchy, Government, Imperial Guard, Imperial Navy, Inquisition, Military, Navigators, Nobility, Rogue Trader, Underworld, Workers

The Explorer suffers an additional −10 on Fellowship Tests with this group. The effect stacks with **Rival**, for a total of −20. GM and player can grant the Talent to fit the campaign; after reconciling with the group it may be removed via an Elite Advance with GM approval.

### Energy Cache
**Prerequisite:** Mechanicus Implants

The Explorer no longer gains Fatigue from Luminen Charge, Luminen Shock and Luminen Blast.

### Enhanced Bionic Frame
**Prerequisite:** Machinator Array

The Explorer gains the **Auto-stabilised Trait** (p. 364).

### Exotic Weapon Training †
**Talent Groups:** all Exotic Weapons

The Talent applies to a single Exotic Weapon and allows its use without penalty.

### Favoured by the Warp
**Prerequisite:** Willpower 35

If a Focus Power Test results in Psychic Phenomena, the Explorer rolls twice and chooses the more favourable result.

### Fearless
The Explorer is immune to Fear and Pinning. To disengage from combat or retreat, however, he must pass a successful Willpower Test.

### Feedback Screech
**Prerequisite:** Mechanicus Implants

All unprotected creatures within a 30-metre radius who can hear the Explorer must make a Willpower Test. On failure, they lose a Half Action on their next turn.

### Ferric Lure
**Prerequisite:** Mechanicus Implants

As a Full Action and with a successful Willpower Test, the Explorer can call an unsecured, visible metal object from up to 20 metres away into his hand. Maximum mass: `1 kg × Willpower Bonus`.

### Ferric Summons
**Prerequisites:** Mechanicus Implants, Ferric Lure

Works like Ferric Lure but up to 40 metres and with a maximum of `2 kg × Willpower Bonus`. Also requires a Full Action and a successful Willpower Test.

### Flame Weapon Training †
**Talent Groups:** Universal

Allows the use of all non-exotic weapons with the **Flame** Special Quality.

### Foresight
**Prerequisite:** Intelligence 30

After 10 minutes of study or analysis of a problem, the Explorer gains +10 on his next Intelligence Test.

### Frenzy
The Explorer can work himself into a rage for a full Round and becomes **Frenzied** at the start of the next Round. He gains +10 to Weapon Skill, Strength, Toughness and Willpower, but −20 to Ballistic Skill and Intelligence. If possible, he must attack the nearest enemy in melee or move toward them; obviously suicidal actions are not required.

While Frenzied he is immune to Fear, Pinning, Stunning and the effects of Fatigue. He cannot Parry, retreat, or flee, and must use **All Out Attack** in melee whenever possible. Frenzy lasts until the end of combat. Psychic Techniques are not permitted during this time unless another Talent explicitly allows it.

### Furious Assault
**Prerequisite:** Weapon Skill 35

If the Explorer hits with an **All Out Attack Action**, he may spend his Reaction to make an additional attack with the same bonuses or penalties.

### Good Reputation †
**Prerequisites:** Fellowship 50, Peer

**Talent Groups:** Academics, Adeptus Arbites, Adeptus Mechanicus, Administratum, Astropaths, Ecclesiarchy, Feral Worlders, Government, Hivers, Imperial Guard, Imperial Navy, Inquisition, Middle Classes, Nobility, the Insane, Underworld, Void Born, Workers

The Explorer gains an additional +10 on Fellowship Tests with the chosen group. The effect stacks with **Peer**, for a total of +20.

### Guardian
**Prerequisite:** Agility 40

The Explorer sacrifices all his Actions for the next Round to swap places with an ally within 2 metres, provided the path is clear. This can happen at any time and can even interrupt another action. The Explorer becomes the target of any attacks that had previously targeted the ally. Usable only once per combat.

### Gun Blessing
**Prerequisite:** Mechanicus Implants

As a Full Action and with a successful Intelligence Test, the Explorer can unjam up to `Intelligence Bonus` jammed weapons within a 10-metre radius.

### Gunslinger
**Prerequisites:** Ballistic Skill 40, Two-Weapon Wielder

With two pistols, the penalty for Two-Weapon Fighting is reduced by 10. If the Explorer also has **Ambidextrous**, the penalty is eliminated entirely.

### Hard Bargain
Whenever Profit Factor is awarded for completing an Endeavour, the group gains an additional +1 Profit Factor.

### Hard Target
**Prerequisite:** Agility 40

After a Charge or Run Action, opponents suffer −20 on Ballistic Skill Tests for Ranged Attacks against the Explorer. The penalty lasts until the start of his next turn.

### Hardy
**Prerequisite:** Toughness 40

During medical treatment and natural healing, the Explorer regenerates Damage as if Lightly Wounded.

### Hatred †
**Talent Groups:** Criminals, Rogue Trader (specific), Pirates, Xeno (specific), Psykers, Mutants

In Close Combat, the Explorer gains +10 on Weapon Skill Tests against members of the chosen group.

### Heavy Weapon Training †
**Talent Groups:** Bolt, Flame, Las, Launcher, Melta, Plasma, Primitive, SP

Weapons of the chosen group can be used without penalty; each further purchase selects another group. Without the matching Weapon Training, −20 applies to relevant Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill Tests.

### Heightened Senses †
**Talent Groups:** Sight, Sound, Smell, Taste, Touch

The Explorer gains +10 on Tests that specifically use the chosen sense. The bonus does not apply generally to actions where the sense is merely involved, such as Ballistic Skill Tests with a Ranged Weapon.

### Hip Shooting
**Prerequisites:** Ballistic Skill 40, Agility 40

As a Full Action, the Explorer may move up to his Full Move Rate and make a single Ranged Attack. The attack must be a Single Shot; Automatic Fire is not allowed.

### Hotshot Pilot
**Prerequisites:** any Pilot Skill, Agility 40

The Talent may only be chosen once the Explorer has a Pilot Skill as an Advanced Skill. All other Pilot Skills count as Basic Skills for him; on Pilot Skills he already has, he gains +10.

### Infused Knowledge
**Prerequisite:** Intelligence 40

All Common Lore and Scholastic Lore Skills count as untrained Basic Skills. On Tests using Common Lore or Scholastic Lore Skills he already possesses, the Explorer gains +10.

### Improved Warp Sense
**Prerequisite:** Warp Sense

The Explorer may use Psyniscience as a Free Action.

### Independent Targeting
**Prerequisite:** Ballistic Skill 40

When firing two weapons as part of the same action, the targets need not be within 10 metres of each other.

### Into the Jaws of Hell
**Prerequisite:** Iron Discipline

In personal combat, visible subordinates are immune to Fear and Pinning. As long as it is known that the Explorer is aboard the ship, its Morale gains +5.

### Inspire Wrath
**Prerequisite:** Fellowship 30

The Explorer gains +20 on Interaction Tests used to stir hatred or anger, and affects double the normal number of people. Combinable with **Master Orator**.

### Iron Discipline
**Prerequisites:** Willpower 30, Command

Subordinates who can see the Explorer personally or via vox/pict-caster may reroll failed Willpower Tests against Fear and Pinning. The Talent affects up to `Willpower Bonus` targets under his command; PCs benefit if he is the official group leader. When leading a Boarding Action (p. 215), he gains +10 on Command Tests.

### Iron Jaw
**Prerequisite:** Toughness 40

If the Explorer is Stunned, he can ignore the effects with a successful Toughness Test.

### Jaded
**Prerequisite:** Willpower 30

Ordinary extreme horrors cause neither Insanity Points nor Fear Tests. Warp-based horrors still function normally.

### Leap Up
**Prerequisite:** Agility 30

The Explorer may stand up as a Free Action.

### Last Man Standing
**Prerequisite:** Nerves of Steel

The Explorer is immune to Pinning from Pistols and Basic Weapons. Cover against Ranged Attacks grants him an additional +1 AP.

### Light Sleeper
**Prerequisite:** Perception 30

For Awareness Tests and Surprise, the Explorer counts as awake even while asleep.

### Lightning Attack
**Prerequisite:** Swift Attack

As a Full Action, the Explorer may make three Melee Attacks; this replaces the effect of Swift Attack. The Talent cannot be combined with **Dual Strike**. With two melee weapons, only one gets Lightning Attack, the other a single attack. With a melee weapon and a gun, the melee weapon gets Lightning Attack and the gun a single attack.

### Lightning Reflexes
On the Initiative roll, the Explorer adds double his Agility Bonus. With **Unnatural Agility**, its multiplier is increased by 1 beforehand.

### Litany of Hate
**Prerequisite:** Hatred (any)

As a Full Action, the Explorer may use a Charm Test to extend the benefit of his Hatred Talent to nearby allies. Success grants a target +10 on Weapon Skill against the hated enemies for each point of Fellowship Bonus. The effect lasts the entire encounter.

### Logis Implant
The Explorer spends his Reaction for the Round and makes a Tech-Use Test. On success, he gains +10 on all Weapon Skill and Ballistic Skill Tests until the end of his next turn. Afterwards, he must pass a Toughness Test or gain 1 Fatigue.

### Luminen Blast
**Prerequisite:** Mechanicus Implants
**Talent Use:** Half Action Attack

A successful Ballistic Skill Test hits a single target within 10 metres and deals `1d10 + Willpower Bonus` Energy Damage. The Explorer must pass a Toughness Test or gain 1 Fatigue.

### Luminen Charge
**Prerequisite:** Mechanicus Implants

After 1 minute of meditation and ritual invocation, the Explorer can use a Toughness Test to charge or power machinery. Difficulty depends on the system:

| Difficulty | Examples |
|---|---|
| Ordinary (+10) | Simple Power Cell, Illuminator |
| Challenging (+0) | Weapon Charge Pack, Data Slate, Bridge Terminal |
| Difficult (−10) | Hotshot Charge Pack, Shuttle Electronics, Servo-Skull |
| Hard (−20) | Lascannon Charge Pack, Servitor, Bridge Hololith |
| Very Hard (−30) | Ship's Cogitator Core, Reactor Machine Spirit, Xenos Tech |

When used, the Explorer must pass a Toughness Test or gain 1 Fatigue. Overly large or alien systems may be unsuitable at the GM's discretion.

### Luminen Shock
**Prerequisite:** Mechanicus Implants
**Talent Use:** Half Action Attack

A successful Weapon Skill Test or Grapple deals `1d10+3` Energy Damage with the **Shocking Quality** (p. 116). The Explorer must pass a Toughness Test or gain 1 Fatigue.

### Machinator Array
**Prerequisite:** Mechanicus Implants

Strength and Toughness increase by +10, Agility and Fellowship decrease by −5. The Explorer's mass triples; he can no longer swim or stay afloat. He may mount a single pistol or Close Combat weapon on any Ballistic Mechadendrite he possesses, but still requires the matching Weapon Training.

### Maglev Grace
**Prerequisite:** Mechanicus Implants

As a Half Action, the Explorer hovers 20–30 cm above the ground for `1d10 + Toughness Bonus` minutes. A Half Action is needed each Round to maintain it; the other action may be used to move normally. When falling, he can slow his descent and reduce falling Damage to `1d10+3 Impact`. Each use drains the Potentia Coil until it is recharged.

### Maglev Transcendence
**Prerequisites:** Mechanicus Implants, Maglev Grace

As a Half Action, the Explorer hovers 20–30 cm for `2d10 + Toughness Bonus` minutes. He must use a Half Action each Round to maintain the rite, but may move up to his running speed. If the rite is active on impact, he takes no falling Damage. Each activation drains 50% of the Potentia Coil.

### Marksman
**Prerequisite:** Ballistic Skill 35

The Explorer suffers no penalties on Ballistic Skill Tests at Long or Extreme Range.

### Master & Commander
**Prerequisites:** Intelligence 35, Fellowship 35

If the Explorer spends a Half Action in combat to direct allies, they suffer no penalties for Ganging Up until his next turn. When defending against a Boarding Action, he may use a Half Action to direct his fighters, giving them +10 in combat (p. 215).

### Master Chirurgeon
**Prerequisite:** Medicae +10

The Explorer gains +10 on all Medicae Tests. On a Heavily or Critically Wounded patient, a successful Test heals 2 Damage instead of 1. If a Critical Hit threatens the loss of a limb, the patient gains +20 on the Toughness Test to prevent it.

### Master Enginseer
**Prerequisites:** Tech-Use +20, Mechanicus or Explorator Implants

For a Fate Point, the Explorer automatically succeeds on a Tech-Use Test to enhance, repair, or upgrade a starship system, and takes the minimum possible time.

### Master Orator
**Prerequisite:** Fellowship 30

Fellowship Tests and Fellowship-based Skill Tests affect ten times the normal number of people.

### Mechadendrite Use †
**Prerequisite:** Explorator
**Talent Groups:** Weapon, Utility

- **Weapon:** Mechadendrites of this type end in ranged or close combat weapons, with the supplemental support and strength needed for combat.
- **Utility:** including such varied types as Machine Spirit Interface, Manipulator, Medicae, Utility and Optical; these generally require less robust mountings but all interface with the Cyber Mantle in a similar manner.

### Meditation
A successful Willpower Test and 10 uninterrupted minutes of meditation remove 1 Fatigue.

### Melee Weapon Training †
**Talent Groups:** Primitive, Universal

**Universal** covers Chain, Shock and Power. Weapons of the chosen group can be used without penalty; without the matching Training, −20 applies to relevant Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill Tests.

### Mighty Shot
**Prerequisite:** Ballistic Skill 40

Ranged Weapons deal +2 Damage.

### Mimic
After at least 1 hour of study, the Explorer can imitate a voice, provided he speaks the same language. Listeners see through the deception with a **Difficult (−10) Scrutiny Test**; if the study was based on vox recordings or comm-link conversations rather than personal observation, the Test is **Challenging (+0)**. It automatically fails if the listener can clearly see the Explorer is not the imitated person. Xenos voices cannot be accurately copied due to physiology and language complexity.

### Navigator
The Explorer possesses the Navigator Gene and a Warp Eye; further rules are in Chapter VII: Navigator Powers.

### Navigator Power
**Prerequisite:** Navigator

The Explorer gains an additional Navigator Power. The Talent can be taken multiple times; each choice grants a new power.

### Nerves of Steel
Failed Willpower Tests to avoid or recover from Pinning may be rerolled.

### Orthoproxy
The Explorer gains +20 on Willpower Tests against Mind Control or Interrogation.

### Paranoia
The Explorer gains +2 to Initiative. The GM may secretly test his Perception so he notices hidden dangers.

### Peer †
**Prerequisite:** Fellowship 30
**Talent Groups:** Academics, Adeptus Arbites, Adeptus Mechanicus, Administratum, Astropaths, Ecclesiarchy, Feral Worlders, Government, Hivers, Inquisition, Middle Classes, Military, Nobility, the Insane, Underworld, Void Born, Workers

The Explorer gains +10 on Fellowship Tests with the chosen group.

### Pistol Weapon Training †
**Talent Groups:** Primitive, Universal

**Universal** covers Bolt, Las, Launcher, Melta, Plasma and SP. Pistols of the chosen group can be used without penalty; without matching Training, −20 applies to relevant Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill Tests.

### Polyglot
**Prerequisites:** Intelligence 40, Fellowship 30

All languages count as Basic Skills. This is not true language mastery; Tests relying on the intuitive, simplified translation suffer −10.

### Precise Blow
**Prerequisites:** Weapon Skill 40, Sure Strike

Called Shots with a melee weapon do not suffer the usual −20 penalty.

### Prosanguine
**Prerequisite:** Autosanguine

After 10 minutes of meditation and ritual invocation, the Explorer may make a Tech-Use Test; on success he heals `1d5` Damage. A roll of 96–100 overloads the implants: for one week, neither Autosanguine nor Prosanguine can be used.

### Psy Rating
The Psy Rating ranges from 1 to 10. The Talent can be taken multiple times and increases the current Psy Rating by 1 each time; this does not automatically grant additional Psychic Powers. Full rules: Chapter VI.

### Psychic Discipline †
**Talent Groups:** Psy Rating

Grants access to a new Psychic Discipline. A psyker can master at most three different Disciplines; see Chapter VI.

### Psychic Technique †
**Talent Groups:** see Psychic Techniques

The Explorer chooses a new Psychic Technique from a mastered Discipline whose XP cost does not exceed the XP cost of this Talent. No additional XP is required. The Talent can be taken multiple times.

### Pure Faith
The Explorer is always immune to **Daemonic Presence**, including its negative Willpower modifiers.

- For a Fate Point, he need not make Fear Tests for the rest of the encounter and gains neither Insanity nor Corruption Points.
- He can burn a Fate Point to completely negate the effect of a single Daemonic or Psychic Attack.

### Purge the Unclean
**Prerequisite:** Pure Faith

- As a Half Action and for a Fate Point, the Explorer makes an Opposed Willpower Test against a Warp Entity. On success, it is pushed back `2 × Willpower Bonus` metres and cannot approach closer than that distance for `2d5` Rounds.
- As a Full Action and for a Fate Point, he can use an Opposed Willpower Test to drive a possessing Warp Entity out of its host. It appears next to the former host and cannot possess it again for `2 × Willpower Bonus` hours.
- As a Full Action, he can burn a Fate Point to directly confront the Warp Entity. A successful Willpower Test deals `Willpower Bonus` Damage plus a further `Willpower Bonus` per Degree of Success. On failure, the Explorer takes Damage equal to his Willpower Bonus. This Damage is reduced by neither Toughness nor Armour.

### Quick Draw
The Explorer can Ready a Pistol, Basic-class Ranged Weapon, or one-handed melee weapon as a Free Action.

### Rapid Reaction
**Prerequisite:** Agility 40

On Surprise or Ambush, the Explorer may act normally with a successful Agility Test.

### Rapid Reload
All reload times are halved and rounded down: a Half Action reload becomes a Free Action, a Full Action becomes a Half Action, and so on.

### Renowned Warrant
The Explorer gains +10 on Interaction Skill Tests with people who understand the significance of his Warrant of Trade, such as other traders and Imperial officials.

### Resistance †
**Talent Groups:** Cold, Fear, Heat, Poisons, Psychic Techniques

Each time it is chosen, a group is set; the Explorer gains +10 on Tests against its effects. Unusual groups require GM approval and should be justified by the character's background.

### Rite of Awe
**Prerequisite:** Explorator

All humans within a 50-metre radius feel dread and suffer −10 on their next Skill Test. A successful Willpower Test ignores the effect. During the two-minute litany, the Explorer cannot communicate; interrupting it is considered very bad form. Humans without auditory implants do not hear the infrasound but are still affected.

### Rite of Fear
**Prerequisite:** Explorator

During the two-minute litany, all humans within a 50-metre radius treat the Explorer as though he has **Fear Rating 1**. He cannot communicate in any other way during this time. Humans without auditory augmentation do not hear the infrasound but are still affected.

### Rite of Pure Thought
**Prerequisite:** Explorator

The Explorer no longer feels emotions and is immune to Fear, Pinning and all effects of emotional disturbance. The GM removes Mental Disorders that no longer apply and grants appropriate new ones of equal severity.

### Rite of Sanctioning
**Prerequisites:** Psy Rating, Special

An individual result on the Psychic Phenomena table (p. 160) is chosen, excluding Perils of the Warp. When the Explorer later rolls for Psychic Phenomena, he may substitute the rolled result with this trademark result, provided he did not roll Perils of the Warp.

### Rival †
**Talent Groups:** Academics, Adepta Sororitas, Adeptus Arbites, Adeptus Mechanicus, Administratum, Astropaths, Ecclesiarchy, Government, Imperial Guard, Imperial Navy, Inquisition, Middle Class, Military, Navigators, Nobility, Rogue Traders, Underworld, Workers

The Explorer suffers −10 on Fellowship Tests with the chosen group. GM and player can grant the Talent to fit the story; after appropriate actions it may be removed via an Elite Advance with GM approval.

### Sharpshooter
**Prerequisites:** Ballistic Skill 40, Deadeye Shot

Called Shots no longer suffer the −20 penalty. This Talent replaces the effect of Deadeye Shot.

### Sound Constitution
The Explorer gains 1 additional Wound. The Talent can be purchased multiple times per Career Advances; the number of purchases is noted, e.g. "Sound Constitution 3".

### Sprint
On a Full Move Action, the Explorer covers additional metres equal to his Agility Bonus. On a Run Action, he may double his movement for 1 Round. Using the Talent in two consecutive turns gives him 1 Fatigue.

### Step Aside
**Prerequisites:** Agility 40, Dodge

The Explorer gains an additional Dodge per Round — effectively a second Reaction usable only for Dodge. Against the same attack, only one Dodge is still allowed.

### Strong Minded
**Prerequisites:** Willpower 30, Resistance (Psychic Techniques)

Failed Willpower Tests against Psychic Techniques that affect the mind may be rerolled. Physical effects such as Telekinesis are not affected.

### Sure Strike
**Prerequisite:** Weapon Skill 30

When determining the Hit Location of a Melee Attack, the Explorer may use the dice in the rolled or reversed order and choose the preferred location.

### Swift Attack
**Prerequisite:** Weapon Skill 35

As a Full Action, the Explorer may make two Melee Attacks. With Two-Weapon Wielder, one melee weapon gets Swift Attack and the other weapon a single attack; this also applies to the combination of melee weapon and gun.

### Takedown
Before the Weapon Skill Test, the Explorer declares a Takedown as a Half Action. If he hits and would deal at least 1 Damage, the Damage is ignored and the target must pass a Toughness Test or be Stunned for 1 Round. During a Stun Action, the Explorer does not suffer the usual −20 penalty to Weapon Skill.

### Talented †
**Talent Groups:** any Skill

The Explorer chooses a Skill and gains +10 on its Tests.

### Technical Knock
**Prerequisite:** Intelligence 30

The Explorer can unjam a touched gun as a Half Action; at most one weapon per Round.

### The Emperor Protects
**Prerequisite:** Pure Faith

- For a Fate Point, the Explorer and up to `Willpower Bonus` allies become immune to Fear and Pinning for the encounter. Ranged and Close Combat Attacks against them suffer −10.
- He can burn a Fate Point so that an ally — never himself — completely negates a single attack. The Fate Point is burned after the hit, but before the Damage roll.

### The Flesh is Weak
**Prerequisite:** Mechanicus Implants

The Explorer gains the **Machine Trait** (p. 365) with Armour Points equal to the number of times this Talent has been taken. Multiple purchases are possible per Career Advances.

### Thrown Weapon Training †
**Talent Groups:** Universal

**Universal** covers all Thrown Weapons from Primitive, Chain, Shock and Power. Without matching Training, −20 applies to relevant Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill Tests.

### Total Recall
**Prerequisite:** Intelligence 30

The Explorer automatically remembers trivial facts and plausible previously acquired information. For detailed, complex or obscure facts, the GM may require an Intelligence Test.

### True Grit
**Prerequisite:** Toughness 40

Critical Damage suffered is halved and rounded up.

### Two-Weapon Wielder †
**Talent Groups:** Ballistic, Melee
**Prerequisites:** Ballistic Skill 35 or Weapon Skill 35, Agility 35

With two weapons of the same type, the Explorer may attack with both as a Full Action; both Attack Tests suffer −20. For the combination of gun and hand weapon, both Two-Weapon Wielder (Melee) and Two-Weapon Wielder (Ballistic) are required.

### Unarmed Master
**Prerequisites:** Weapon Skill 45, Agility 40, Unarmed Warrior

Unarmed Combat Attacks deal `1d10 + Strength Bonus` Impact Damage and lose the Primitive Quality.

### Unarmed Warrior
**Prerequisites:** Weapon Skill 35, Agility 35

Unarmed Combat Attacks deal `1d10−3 + Strength Bonus` Damage instead of `1d5−3`. Against armed opponents, the Explorer does not count as Unarmed (p. 245); the attacks retain the Primitive Quality.

### Unremarkable
Attempts to notice the Explorer in a crowd, describe him, or recall details about him suffer −20.

### Unshakeable Faith
Failed Willpower Tests against Fear may be rerolled.

### Void Tactician
**Prerequisite:** Intelligence 35

The Explorer gains +10 on Ballistic Skill Tests used to fire a starship's guns in combat.

### Wall of Steel
**Prerequisite:** Agility 35

The Explorer gains an additional Parry per Round — effectively a second Reaction usable only for Parry. Against the same attack, only one Parry is still allowed.

### Warp Affinity
**Prerequisite:** Psy Rating

The Talent cannot be chosen after Rite of Sanctioning. When rolling for Psychic Phenomena, the Explorer may discard the result, gain `1d5` Corruption Points, and reroll with no modifiers.

### Warp Conduit
**Prerequisites:** Psy Rating, Strong Minded, Willpower 50

When Pushing, the Explorer may add a further +1 to his Psy Rating and subtract 10 from any resulting Psychic Phenomena roll.

### Warp Sense
**Prerequisites:** Navigator or Psy Rating, Psyniscience, Perception 30

Psyniscience requires only a Half Action instead of a Full Action.

### Whispers
**Prerequisites:** Intelligence 45, Fellowship 35

The Explorer gains +10 on any Skill Test for Investigation, as well as on the **Interview** Special Use of the Inquiry Skill.

### Wrath of the Righteous
**Prerequisite:** Pure Faith

- On an attack, the Explorer may spend a Fate Point to deal an additional `1d5` Damage.
- During an attack, he may burn a Fate Point to trigger Righteous Fury on a single successful hit. The attack automatically deals the maximum weapon Damage plus `1d10` Damage; if this second roll shows a 10, further Damage is possible per the rules for Righteous Fury (p. 245).
